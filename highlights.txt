## 🔍 Why It Matters

### The Problem
Standard physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) exhibit **uncontrolled invariant drift** in conservative systems. Over long integration horizons, energy/momentum invariants diverge, destroying physical fidelity.

### The Solution
**Multiplicative structural correction** (Eq. 3):

ẑ = z_θ · exp(-λ(H(z_θ) - H₀))

- Projects outputs toward invariant manifold at every forward pass
- Decouples dynamical accuracy from geometric fidelity
- No expensive root-finding or hyperparameter tuning

### The Impact
- **4.5× greater improvement** than soft penalty methods
- **Statistical rigor:** n=15 independent runs, B=10,000 bootstrap replicates
- **Reproducibility:** deterministic seeding, complete data archival

## 📊 Statistical Validation Protocol (Published)

| Aspect | Implementation |
|--------|---------------|
| Sample size | n = 15 independent runs |
| Randomization | Deterministic seeding (seed = 2 + i) |
| Comparison | Three-way: Baseline / Soft IP-PINN / SC-PINN |
| Significance test | Welch's t-test (α = 0.05, unequal variances) |
| Effect size | Cohen's d |
| Confidence intervals | Bootstrap (B = 10,000 replicates) |
| Normality | Shapiro-Wilk (honest reporting: Baseline p=0.191; Soft p=0.004; SC p<0.001) |
| Non-parametric | Mann-Whitney U + Cliff's delta (Appendix B) |

**Result:** All main claims confirmed under both parametric and non-parametric validation. Soft penalty improvement is indistinguishable from noise (p = 0.063); structural correction is definitive (p < 10⁻¹¹).

## 🔬 New Analyses (Major Revision)

### 1. Sensitivity Analysis (Figure 6)
Systematic evaluation of projection strength λ ∈ [0.01, 2.0]:
- **Robust region:** λ ∈ [0.05, 0.5] yields comparable performance
- **Selected value:** λ = 0.1 (conservative middle ground)
- **Instability:** λ > 1.0 introduces training stiffness

### 2. Long-Time Integration (Figure 5)
Models trained on [0,100], evaluated on [0,500]:
- **SC-PINN:** Maintains bounded deviation (stable behavior)
- **Baseline:** Exhibits unbounded drift

### 3. Trajectory Confidence (Figure 2 revised)
Maximum Euclidean distance from RK4 over T=50 (n=15):

| Method | Max Distance | Mean Distance |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Baseline PINN | 2.34 ± 0.41 | 0.89 ± 0.15 |
| **SC-PINN** | **0.87 ± 0.19** | **0.31 ± 0.08** |

### 4. Ablation Study (Appendix A, n=15)
Comparison of full model vs. architectural correction alone:

| Configuration | Mean ± SD | Welch p | Cohen's d |
|---------------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Full model | 0.922 ± 0.112 | — | — |
| Without refinement | 0.919 ± 0.115 | 0.935 | 0.03 |

**Conclusion:** The architectural correction alone provides the dominant invariant stabilization. The refinement loss serves as an auxiliary optimization stabilizer during training.

## 🧠 Architectural Insights

### Why Soft Penalties Fail
1. **Competing objectives:** Invariant loss vs. ODE residual trade off
2. **Weight sensitivity:** λ tuning critical, system-dependent
3. **Approximate satisfaction:** Violations proportional to λ

### Why Structural Correction Succeeds
1. **Explicit feedback:** Direct geometric control at output layer
2. **Optimization decoupling:** Architecture handles invariants, loss handles dynamics
3. **Continuous stabilization:** Prevents error accumulation over time

### Computational Efficiency
- Overhead: ~3% (847s vs. 822s per run)
- vs. Soft penalties: ~5-10× tuning cost
- vs. Exact projection: Orders of magnitude faster (no root-finding)

## 🔬 Experimental Design

### System
Lotka-Volterra predator-prey (conservative ODE):
- Parameters: α=1.0, β=0.1, δ=0.075, γ=0.75
- Initial condition: (2.0, 2.0) → H₀ ≈ -0.863
- Integration: T = 50 time units

### Network
- Base: Feedforward (log-population prediction)
- Activation: Exponential (positivity guarantee)
- Correction: Scalar multiplicative nudge (λ = 0.1)

### Training
- Loss: L_ODE + λ_IC·L_IC + 0.5·E[(H(ẑ) - H₀)²]
- Ablation: Refinement term is auxiliary (not statistically significant in isolation)
- Hardware: Standard consumer GPU (reproducible on CPU)

## ⚠️ Limitations & Future Work

| Limitation | Current State | Extension Path |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| Known invariants | Required analytically | Learned invariants from data |
| Fixed λ | Robust but suboptimal | Adaptive/meta-learned strength |
| Asymptotic correction | Preserves value, not symplectic form | Symplectic neural networks for canonical systems |
| ODE focus | Finite-dimensional invariants | PDEs with infinite-dimensional invariants |
